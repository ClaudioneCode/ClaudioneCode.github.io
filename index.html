LoL Companion

A free Windows desktop companion for League of Legends players. Understand your performance and improve, in a clean and modern interface.

My Profile

Link your Riot ID (or let the app detect the account logged into the League client) to see ranked status, match history and performance metrics: damage per minute, CS per minute, kill participation, vision and champion mastery.

Live game

See who is in your current game: champions, summoner spells, keystone rune and rank. Only information already visible in the loading screen.

Leaderboards

Challenger, Grandmaster and Master ladders per region and queue.

Match analysis

Post-game review against your lane opponent with practical tips.

Champions & patches

Champion browser, tier list and patch overview.

Privacy

LoL Companion does not store match or profile data on any server. Data is requested from the Riot Games API only when you open a page. The only information saved is the Riot ID you choose to link, stored locally on your own PC. We do not sell or share any data. Contact: claudio.dilecce.lavoro@gmail.com
