Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who we are

Better Stomp is an independent desktop app for League of Legends. Contact: claudio.dilecce.lavoro@gmail.com.

Data stored on your PC

  • Your settings, the Riot ID you choose to link and recent searches are saved locally on your computer. You can delete them by uninstalling the app.
  • The app reads the League client (LCU) and the Live Client Data API only on your own PC, to show your profile, import runes and items, and run the overlay. This data is not uploaded.

Data processed by our server

  • Riot API requests. The app asks our server to fetch public data from the Riot Games API (profiles, ranks, match history). The server forwards the request and returns the answer. It does not keep a copy.
  • Champion statistics. Our server reads public ranked matches from the Riot Games API and keeps only aggregated, anonymous totals per champion and role (games, wins, items, runes, matchups). To avoid reading the same match twice, it stores match IDs and the encrypted player identifiers (PUUIDs) issued by Riot. It stores no names, Riot IDs or chat.
  • Coach AI. When you ask for a review, the game statistics shown in the app are sent through our server to an AI provider (Groq or Anthropic) to generate the answer. The request does not include your Riot ID. Answers are not stored, except general patch summaries shared by all users.
  • IP address. Your IP address is used only for short-term rate limiting to prevent abuse. It is not logged or stored.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics.
  • We do not access the League client store, chat or purchases.

Service providers

Riot Games (game data), Cloudflare (server hosting and database), Groq and Anthropic (Coach AI). Each processes data under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to access or delete any data related to you, including removing your PUUID from the statistics database, by writing to the contact above. If you live in the EU, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
