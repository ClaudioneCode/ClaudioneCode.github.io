Better Stomp

A free Windows desktop companion for League of Legends players. Understand your own performance and improve, in a clean and modern interface. Available in English and Italian.

Profile

Detects the account logged into the League client, or link any Riot ID. Ranked status, LP, your last 20 games, main roles, champion mastery, and performance metrics: damage per minute, CS per minute, kill participation and vision. A Premade tab shows the friends you queued with most.

Match analysis

Post-game review against your lane opponent: what went well first, then what to improve, with practical tips.

Champions & builds

Champion pages with runes, starting items, core build and situational items, based on aggregated ranked games. Tier list and patch overview.

Champion select

Imports your chosen rune page and item set into the client with one click (only pages the app created, prefixed "BS:"). Suggests picks for your role by lane matchup or team composition. You always make the final choice.

In-game overlay

Your own CS per minute against a personal goal, and the team item-value difference while you hold Tab. It only uses the official Live Client Data API and information already visible in game. No memory reading, no enemy timers.

Streaming

A browser-source widget for OBS and Twitch with your rank, LP and session record.

Coach AI

Ask for an AI review of a finished game, or for a game plan when a match starts, based only on the champions shown in the loading screen. It does not follow or react to what happens during the match.

Free and Premium

Every feature above is free. A future optional Premium subscription will add more Coach AI reviews, weekly progress reports and longer match history. No ads inside the League client or the game, and no betting or gambling features.
